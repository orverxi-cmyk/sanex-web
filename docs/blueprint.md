# **App Name**: Sanex Core

## Core Features:

- Service Portfolio Interface: Dynamic catalog of solutions including Liquid Waste Collection, Decentralized Wastewater Treatment Systems (DWTS), and expert consultancy.
- AI Waste Solutions Architect tool: An intelligent diagnostic tool that uses business sector (hospital, school, hotel) and capacity data to recommend optimal sanitation systems and schedules.
- Milestone Evolution Tracker: Interactive timeline displaying SANEX journey from the 2017 foundation to the 2024 regional expansion.
- Regional Availability Portal: Interface highlighting operational capacities across Kigali, Musanze, Huye, Nyagatare, and Rwamagana.
- Impact Dashboard: A visualized data module showcasing environmental preservation statistics and public health outcomes achieved since 2017.
- Intelligent Quote Engine: A streamlined multi-step lead capture system that helps clients obtain immediate estimates for collection and transport services.
- Sustainable Partnerships Directory: UI gallery displaying SANEX collaboration with over 114 government and private clients nationwide.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Professional Azure (#2D94B8) representing clean water and modern industrial reliability.
- Background: Ultra-light Arctic Frost (#EBF6F9) to provide a sanitized, fresh, and modern feel throughout the application.
- Accent: Deep Mint (#2D9E75) providing high contrast for calls-to-action, signifying sustainable environmental growth and public health.
- Headlines use 'Space Grotesk' for a technical, precise, and advanced technology vibe; body text uses 'Inter' for high readability and professional neutrality.
- Line-based, minimal iconography featuring scientific icons for treatment systems and geometric symbols for logistics and transport.
- Minimalist grid system with generous white space and asymmetrical photo placements of treatment plants for an award-winning editorial look.
- Smooth, sequential staggered entries for statistics and content blocks to evoke a sense of professional precision.
